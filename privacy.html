Privacy Policy

KAI Social Analytics is an independent educational prototype, not an official service of PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero).

1. Information Collected

With user authorization, the application may access TikTok profile information, video metadata, and engagement statistics, depending on approved API permissions.

2. Use of Information

Information is used only for testing, displaying analytics, and evaluating social media performance.

3. Data Storage

During prototype development, authorized data may be processed locally. Access tokens and client secrets are kept server-side and are not intentionally published.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell user information or intentionally share it with advertisers. TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Test data is retained only as necessary for development and should be deleted when no longer required. Users may revoke TikTok authorization through their TikTok account settings.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the developer through the project repository. Do not post sensitive information publicly.
